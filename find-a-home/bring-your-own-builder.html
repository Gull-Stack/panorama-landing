How Bring Your Own Builder Works

Three simple steps stand between you and a custom home in the Herriman foothills.

1. Reserve Your Homesite

Tour the available lots, pick the village and view that speaks to you, and place a reservation to hold your homesite while you plan.

2. Choose Your Builder

Bring the custom builder you already know and trust, or ask us for an introduction to builders who have worked in the community.

3. Design & Build

Work with your builder and our design guidelines to create a home that fits your homesite, your lifestyle, and the foothill setting.
