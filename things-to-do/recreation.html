Around Herriman

Recreation & Sports

From world-class skiing to championship golf — Utah's playground is your backyard.

The Greatest Snow on Earth

World-Class Skiing

Utah is legendary for its powder — and Panorama puts you within 45 minutes of some of the best skiing on the planet. With 15 resorts within 90 minutes, you'll never run out of terrain to explore.

From the legendary steeps of Snowbird and Alta to the groomed perfection of Deer Valley, there's a resort for every skill level and preference.

Top Resorts:

Park City Mountain — 45 min
Deer Valley — 50 min
Brighton — 40 min
Solitude — 42 min
Snowbird — 50 min
Alta — 55 min
Year-Round Play

Championship Golf

With 300+ days of sunshine and courses stretching from valley floors to mountain plateaus, Utah offers exceptional golf year-round. The South Valley alone has over a dozen courses within 30 minutes of Panorama.

Nearby Courses:

  • Stonebridge Golf Club — 15 min, par 72
  • South Mountain Golf Course — 20 min, par 72
  • Thanksgiving Point Golf Club — 20 min, par 72
  • Glenmoor Golf Course — 18 min, par 70
  • Mountain Dell — 30 min, two 18-hole courses

Water Recreation

Lakes and reservoirs for boating, fishing, and beach days.

Oquirrh Lake (Daybreak)

10 min from Panorama

65-acre lake with beach access, kayaking, paddleboarding, and swimming. Annual triathlon venue.

Utah Lake State Park

30 min from Panorama

Utah's largest freshwater lake. Boating, fishing, sailing, and camping. Boat rentals available.

Jordanelle Reservoir

45 min from Panorama

Mountain reservoir near Park City. Wakeboarding, waterskiing, and fishing with stunning views.
