Bluffdale Utah

Bluffdale offers new construction in a growing area — but for mountain views that take your breath away, Panorama is just minutes south.

Bluffdale New Construction

Bluffdale sits at the southern tip of Salt Lake County, offering a semi-rural feel while maintaining easy access to urban amenities. New construction in Bluffdale has increased as the city develops, particularly around the Point of the Mountain area.

While Bluffdale offers some attractive new home options, most developments are on the valley floor without the elevated mountain views that define premium Utah living.

For buyers seeking Bluffdale new construction but wanting panoramic Wasatch views, Panorama in neighboring Herriman delivers an exceptional alternative — with six premium builders, world-class amenities, and views that Bluffdale simply can't match.

The Panorama Advantage

Just 15 minutes from Bluffdale, Panorama offers what valley-floor new construction cannot:

  • Foothills elevation above the valley floor
  • Master-planned amenities including Panorama Park
