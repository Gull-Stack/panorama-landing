Parks & Open Space

Foothills on One Side, the Valley on the Other

Herriman sits at the base of the Oquirrh Mountains. Butterfield Canyon opens on the west side of town, and downtown Salt Lake City is about 25 minutes the other way.

  • Butterfield Canyon – opens on the west side of Herriman, into the Oquirrh Mountains.
  • Rose Canyon – in the mountains above Butterfield.
  • Oquirrh Lake – Daybreak, about 10 minutes. The path around the lake is public.
  • Mountain View Corridor Trail – runs along the corridor beside Herriman.

Panorama's own trail system is listed with the amenities.
