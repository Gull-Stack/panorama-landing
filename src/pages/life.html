Signature Amenity

Panorama Park

This isn't a token playground. At the heart of the community is Panorama Park, a three-level experience built into the hillside with lit towers, slides connecting each level, and the kind of design that makes your home the place every kid wants to visit.

  • Three-level playground
  • 75-ft double zip line
  • Mighty Descent hill slide
  • Double-decker cone spinner
  • Unity ring spinner
  • Hoopla multi-user swing
  • Traditional swing sets
  • Lit towers at night
