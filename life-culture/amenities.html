Play at Every Level

World-class amenities designed for all ages — from toddlers to grandparents, everyone has their place at Panorama.

Signature Amenity

Panorama Park

This isn't a token playground. At the heart of the community is Panorama Park — a three-level experience built into the hillside with iconic lit towers, slides connecting each level, and the kind of design that makes your home the place every kid wants to visit.

Trail System

Miles of connected loops winding through every neighborhood and up into the foothills. See the trail system →

Dog Park

Off-leash play and socializing with fenced areas for large and small dogs — your four-legged family deserves the view too.

Tot Lot

A dedicated play area for ages 2-4 with whimsical structures, shared swings, and shaded spots for parents to stay close.

Gathering Pavilions

Covered pavilions for birthday parties, neighborhood barbecues, book clubs, or just escaping the sun while the kids play.

Pocket Parks

Small parks scattered through each village create gathering spots just steps from your door, with benches, shade trees, and open lawn.
