Built for Connection

In an age of screens and schedules, Panorama is designed to bring people together. Pocket parks scattered throughout the community invite spontaneous gatherings. Wide sidewalks encourage evening walks with neighbors. Gathering pavilions host birthday parties, book clubs, and neighborhood barbecues.
