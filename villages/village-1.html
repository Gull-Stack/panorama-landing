Village 1 at Panorama

Premium homesites in Herriman's most sought-after foothills location. Experience panoramic Wasatch Mountain views from every lot.

Panorama Herriman Homes in Village 1

Village 1 represents the pinnacle of Panorama living. Situated at the heart of our master-planned community, these homesites offer unobstructed views of the Wasatch Mountains and direct trail access to Panorama Park.

Whether you're seeking a modern farmhouse townhome or a spacious single-family residence, Village 1 provides the perfect canvas for your Utah dream home. With lots ranging from cottage-sized to estate proportions, there's a homesite to match every lifestyle.

Why Choose Panorama Herriman?

Panorama is Herriman's last foothills community — meaning these mountain view homesites won't come again. Village 1 offers the perfect balance of accessibility and elevation, with quick access to Mountain View Village shopping while maintaining that elevated, away-from-it-all feeling.

New construction in Herriman has never looked this good. Our builders maintain elevated design standards, ensuring every home reflects the quality and craftsmanship this location deserves.
